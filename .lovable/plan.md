

# JokeCrafter – AI Joke Generator

A sleek, modern joke generator that creates witty, punchy jokes based on your topic and style preferences.

---

## 1. Core Experience

**Main Interface**
- Dark themed, gradient-accented design with smooth animations
- Central input field for topic/prompt (e.g., "programmers", "cats", "Mondays")
- Style dropdown selector with options:
  - Dad Joke (wholesome puns)
  - Sarcastic (dry wit)
  - Absurd (surreal humor)
  - Dark (edgy but clean)
  - One-liner (quick zingers)
- Large "Generate" button with loading animation
- Joke display card with setup → punchline formatting

---

## 2. Interaction Features

**Regenerate Button**
- Quick regeneration with same topic/style settings
- Dice icon with spin animation on click

**Copy & Share**
- One-click copy to clipboard with success toast
- Share buttons for X (Twitter) and WhatsApp
- Native share on mobile devices

---

## 3. History Sidebar

**Sliding Panel**
- Collapsible sidebar showing recently generated jokes
- Each history item shows topic, style, and preview
- Click to view full joke again
- Clear history option
- Persisted in browser local storage

---

## 4. AI Integration

**Lovable AI (Gemini)**
- Edge function for joke generation with carefully crafted prompts
- Prompts engineered for short, punchy jokes (1-3 sentences max)
- Enforced setup → punchline structure
- Style-specific prompt variations
- Response streaming for instant feel

---

## 5. Visual Design

**Dark & Modern Theme**
- Deep dark background (#0f0f1a) with subtle gradients
- Accent colors: electric purple/blue gradients
- Glassmorphism effects on cards
- Smooth fade and scale animations
- Modern sans-serif typography
- Subtle glow effects on interactive elements

---

## 6. Mobile Experience

- Fully responsive design
- History as bottom sheet on mobile
- Touch-optimized buttons and inputs
- Share via native mobile share sheet
